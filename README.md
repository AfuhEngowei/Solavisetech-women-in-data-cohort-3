# Scolavise Women in Data (SWID) Program

## About the Program

The **Scolavise Women in Data (SWID)** program is a 12-week intensive training program designed to introduce participants to the world of data and help them develop practical skills. It provides an opportunity to learn about data, explore relevant tools and technologies, and begin understanding how data can be used to solve real-world problems.

I see this program as an opportunity to explore a new field, discover my potential, and begin building the knowledge and skills needed for a future career in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Machine Learning (ML).

## What I Aim to Achieve by the End of the Program

I joined the Scolavise Women in Data program with excitement, curiosity, hope, and a strong desire to learn. At the same time, I feel a little nervous because I am beginning a journey in a field I know very little about. I currently have little to no knowledge of data science, AI, or machine learning, and there is still so much for me to discover.

Although the field may seem unfamiliar and challenging at the moment, I am excited about the possibility of learning something new and developing skills that could shape my future career. I know that I may encounter difficult concepts, make mistakes, and sometimes feel overwhelmed by the amount of information I need to understand. However, I am willing to be patient with myself, ask questions, practice consistently, and learn from my mistakes.

One of the things I hope to gain from this program is confidence. I want to move from being someone who knows very little about data to someone who understands its basic concepts, recognizes its importance, and has the confidence to continue learning independently. I want to look back at the end of these 12 weeks and see evidence of how far I have come.

By the end of the program, I aim to:

- **Understand the fundamentals of Data Science:** Learn what data science is, why it matters, and how data can be collected, organized, processed, analyzed, and interpreted to generate useful insights.
- **Gain foundational knowledge of Artificial Intelligence:** Understand what AI means, how it works at a basic level, and how it is used to solve problems and support decision-making in different fields.
- **Explore Machine Learning:** Develop an introductory understanding of machine learning, how computers identify patterns in data, and how these patterns can be used to make predictions or support decisions.
- **Develop practical technical skills:** Become familiar with relevant tools, technologies, and basic techniques introduced during the program and understand how they can be applied when working with data.
- **Gain hands-on experience:** Apply what I learn through assignments, exercises, and practical activities so that I can move beyond simply knowing concepts to beginning to use them.
- **Improve my problem-solving abilities:** Learn how to approach problems logically, ask meaningful questions, interpret information, and understand how data can help people make informed decisions.
- **Discover my interests within the data field:** Explore Data Science, AI, and Machine Learning to understand these areas better and identify which aspects I would like to study more deeply as I develop my career.
- **Build a foundation for my future career:** Gain enough introductory knowledge and practical exposure to understand the next steps I need to take, the skills I need to develop, how I can continue growing in the field after the program ends and also gain valuable connections that will help me in my carrier as a girl or woman in data.

## My Expectations and Hopes

More than anything, I hope this program gives me a solid starting point. I do not expect to become an expert in just 12 weeks, and I understand that building a career in data will require continuous learning, practice, and dedication beyond the program.

However, I want these 12 weeks to make a meaningful difference in my understanding of the field. I want to become more comfortable with data-related concepts, understand the purpose of the tools I encounter, and begin connecting what I learn to real-world applications.

I am hopeful that the program will open my mind to new possibilities and help me see that, even as a beginner, I can gradually develop the skills needed to work in this field. I want to finish the program feeling more knowledgeable, more confident, and better prepared to take the next step toward my career goals.

By the end of these 12 weeks, my goal is to move from having little to no knowledge of Data Science, AI, and Machine Learning to possessing a foundational understanding of these fields and a clear direction for continuing my learning journey and building a career in data.
